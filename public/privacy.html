KeyShortcut Mac app

The Mac app is built with privacy as a core principle and collects no personal data. Everything in this section applies to the app only.

Data Collection

The Mac app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no analytics frameworks, no tracking pixels, no third-party SDKs, and no advertising.

Network Access

KeyShortcut connects to Supabase (our database provider) to download shortcut data. These requests are read-only and keep your shortcut library up to date. No personal data, device identifiers, or usage information is sent. Without a connection, the app uses its built-in and cached shortcuts.

The app also uses Apple's MetricKit framework for anonymized crash reporting (see Apple Diagnostics below). It makes no other network requests.

Local Storage

KeyShortcut stores your preferences locally on your device using macOS UserDefaults within the app sandbox. This includes:

  • Favorited shortcuts
  • Custom shortcuts you create
  • Window position and appearance settings
  • Language preference
  • Imported shortcut pack file references

This data never leaves your Mac. Uninstalling KeyShortcut removes all associated data.

Accessibility Permission

KeyShortcut does not request macOS Accessibility permission and does not monitor your keystrokes.

Active App Detection (optional, off by default) reads only the bundle identifier of the frontmost app, using standard macOS APIs, to show relevant shortcuts. No window content is accessed.

Apple Diagnostics

KeyShortcut uses Apple's MetricKit framework, which allows Apple to collect anonymized crash reports and performance metrics through the standard macOS diagnostics pipeline. This data is processed by Apple, not by us. You can control this in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children's Privacy

KeyShortcut does not collect any data from any user, including children.
